    What the score means. Criticality is how loudly a story is being talked about, on a 0 to 100 scale. It blends platform heat (Hacker News points and comments against the day's front-page maximum, Ground News source counts, visible trends), breadth (how many independent major outlets carry it) and velocity (still escalating today or peaked days ago). The raw inputs sit in each card's small print. It is not an importance score.

    Bands: 85 and up Critical · 70 to 84 High · 40 to 69 Moderate · under 40 Low. A card marked unverified rests on a single source or on a claimant's own statement.
